How it works

Dual-layer security: motion sensing plus RFID authorization.

1. Time window

The DS3231 RTC arms monitoring from 18:00 to 07:00, including across midnight.

2. Motion detected

The HC-SR501 PIR sensor starts a 5-second window to present an RFID card.

3. RFID check

The RC522 reader compares the card UID with the authorized card.

4. Alarm

No valid card: LED on and buzzer beeps for 5 seconds. Authorized card: alarm suppressed.
